# Enterprise Data Warehouse & Analytics Platform

## Overview
Welcome to the **Data Warehouse & Analytics** repository. 

This project showcases an end-to-end data warehousing and analytics solution designed to transform raw operational data into actionable business intelligence. Built to mirror production-grade standards, it highlights modern best practices in **Data Architecture, ETL/ELT Pipelines, Dimensional Modeling, and Analytics**.

## 🚀 Project Requirements & Scope

This project is divided into two core phases: **Data Engineering** (building the foundation) and **Data Analytics** (extracting insights).

### 🏗️ Phase 1: Data Engineering (Data Warehouse Build)
**Objective:** 
Architect and deploy a centralized modern data warehouse using **SQL Server** to unify sales data, creating a single source of truth for business intelligence and reporting.

**Key Specifications:**
- **Source Systems Integration:** Extract and load raw data originating from disparate systems (ERP and CRM), provided as flat CSV files.
- **Data Transformation & Cleansing:** Implement rigorous data quality checks to identify, cleanse, and resolve inconsistencies prior to the modeling phase.
- **Data Modeling:** Design an optimized, analytical-friendly data model that seamlessly merges ERP and CRM data for high-performance querying.
- **Temporal Scope:** The warehouse is designed for current-state analysis (focusing on the latest dataset); historical tracking (SCDs) is out of scope for this iteration.
- **Data Dictionary & Documentation:** Deliver comprehensive documentation of the schema and data model to ensure seamless collaboration between technical teams and business stakeholders.

---

### 📊 Phase 2: Business Intelligence & Analytics
**Objective:** 
Leverage SQL to construct robust analytical queries that translate raw data into actionable business intelligence.

**Focus Areas:**
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Customer Behavior Analysis:** Understanding purchasing patterns and customer segmentation.
- 📦 **Product Performance Metrics:** Identifying top-performing products and inventory movement.
- 📈 **Sales Trend Forecasting:** Analyzing revenue growth, seasonality, and overall sales trajectories.

*Goal: To empower executive stakeholders with critical KPIs and metrics, driving data-informed strategic decisions.*
